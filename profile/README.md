# Environmental Data, Engineering & Climate Tech

A collection of projects focused on the intersection of **environmental engineering, data analytics, air quality, climate technology, and machine learning**.

This organization serves as a portfolio and workspace for practical projects that use data and technology to better understand environmental systems and support evidence-based decision-making.

## Approach

The goal is not simply to apply technology to environmental problems, but to connect:

**Environmental Science → Engineering → Data → Machine Learning → Practical Insight**

Projects are developed with an emphasis on clear methodology, reproducibility, interpretable results, and real-world environmental applications.
